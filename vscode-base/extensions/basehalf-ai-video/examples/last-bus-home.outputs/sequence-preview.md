动作：信封边角被风轻轻掀动两次。雨滴持续落下。林遥的脚从画面左侧进入后停住，不切镜。

A young woman's dark sneakers enter from frame left and stop beside it. Cool rain shadows, one warm practical lamp, realistic live action, subtle film grain. Continuous seamless five-second shot.
