## How to sign

You sign **once**, on your first pull request — there is nothing to print or
mail:

1. Open a pull request as usual.
2. The **CLA Assistant** bot will comment on the PR with a link to this document.
3. Reply on the PR with the exact sentence the bot asks for:

   > I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

4. The bot records your GitHub username and the signed version. Your PR's CLA
   check turns green. Future PRs are covered automatically.

If you ever need to re-trigger the check, comment `recheck` on the PR.

## Corporate contributions

If you are contributing **as part of your employment** or otherwise on behalf of
a company, your employer should sign a **Corporate CLA** so the company's
contributions are covered. Email **chouarslan@gmail.com** (subject: "BaseHalf
Corporate CLA") and we will send the corporate form.

## If you would rather not sign

That's completely fine — we still welcome **issues, bug reports, ideas, and
feedback**. Only contributions of code or other copyrightable material require a
signed CLA before they can be merged.
