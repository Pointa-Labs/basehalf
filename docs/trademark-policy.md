# BaseHalf trademark & brand-usage policy

> **Draft.** Becomes fully enforceable once the "BaseHalf" mark is filed. Have
> counsel review before relying on it. This is the mechanism that lets BaseHalf
> keep **one brand** for the open and commercial editions while still protecting
> it — see [decisions.md](decisions.md) D10.

## The short version

**The code is open (Apache-2.0). The name and logo are not.**

You may freely use, modify, fork, and redistribute the BaseHalf *source code*
under the Apache-2.0 license. But "BaseHalf", the BaseHalf logo, and confusingly
similar names/marks are trademarks of **Pointa Labs, Inc.**, and the Apache-2.0
license **does not grant trademark rights** (see its Section 6).

This is the same arrangement used by Chromium/Chrome, Firefox, and VS
Code/VSCodium: anyone can build from the open source, but **builds you distribute
must carry their own name**, not ours.

## What you can do without asking

- Use the name "BaseHalf" to **refer** to the official project (nominative use):
  "a plugin for BaseHalf", "works with BaseHalf", blog posts, talks, tutorials.
- Fork the repository and develop on it.
- Distribute **unmodified** official releases under their original name.

## What requires a different name (no permission needed — just rename)

If you distribute a **modified build** or a **fork** to others, give it your own
distinct name and branding. Don't call your build "BaseHalf", don't use the
logo, and don't name it in a way that implies it's official or endorsed
(e.g. "BaseHalf Plus", "BaseHalf Pro", "BaseHalf-NG").

## What requires our written permission

- Using "BaseHalf" or the logo in **your product, company, or domain name**.
- Any use suggesting **official endorsement, affiliation, or certification**.
- Merchandise.

## Reserved for Pointa Labs, Inc.

The commercial editions — **BaseHalf Cloud** and **BaseHalf for Teams** (hosted
sync, team spaces, SSO/admin) — and the official binaries and brand are operated
by Pointa Labs, Inc. The open core is, and will remain, Apache-2.0.
